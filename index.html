Patients from abroad

Dental care for international patients, planned before you travel

If you live outside India, start with a video consultation and know the plan and the cost before you book any travel.

  1. 01

    Send your records

    Message on WhatsApp from any country — include your country code — with your X-ray or scan, photographs and a short note on the problem.

  2. 02

    Video consultation

    The doctor reviews your case, explains your options and tells you whether in-person treatment is advisable and roughly how many visits it may need.

  3. 03

    Written plan, then visit

    You receive the proposed treatment and costs in writing before you travel. Appointments are in Hyderabad or Davangere.

Consultation fee ₹300 (Indian rupees); any treatment is priced separately and agreed in writing first. To begin, choose “Teledentistry” in the booking form below.

About the doctor

Specialist care, explained simply

A short introduction, followed by a record of how his career was built.

Prof. Dr. Venu Gopal Seemakurthi is a humble, kind human being first and a dental specialist second. A Consultant Dental Specialist with an M.D.S. (Master of Dental Surgery), he graduated in dentistry from GDCH, Hyderabad, in 1988 and holds master’s degrees in Prosthodontics and Implantology.
